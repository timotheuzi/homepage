⚔️ Battle Chess User Guide

Welcome to Battle Chess, the game where chess rules meet cartoon violence! This guide will help you set up your first game and master the "bloody" art of cartoon warfare.

Game Summary

Battle Chess is not just a game of strategy; it's a spectacle. Every piece is a character with its own idle bobbing and breathing animations. Moves aren't just slides—they are hops and leaps. Captures aren't just removals—they are visceral, screen-shaking battles that end in unique piece-specific fatalities.

How to Play

Moving Pieces

  1. Select a Piece: Tap any of your pieces.
  2. View Legal Moves: Green Circles (Move), Red Outlines (Capture).
  3. Confirm Move: Tap a highlighted square.
  4. Undo: Use the Undo button.

Battles & Fatalities

When you move your piece onto a red-highlighted square, a Fight is triggered. The board will flicker black at the moment of impact, and the victim will undergo a unique death animation.

Setting Up Games

Access the Settings (gear icon) to choose your game mode:

  • Playing the Computer: Random or Basic difficulty.
  • Local Head-to-Head: Play against someone on the same device.
  • Local Network Multiplayer: Play across two devices on the same Wi-Fi/LAN.

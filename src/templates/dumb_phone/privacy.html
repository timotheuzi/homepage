PRIVACY POLICY

YOUR PRIVACY MATTERS

THIS APPLICATION IS DESIGNED WITH PRIVACY AS A FUNDAMENTAL PRINCIPLE. NOTHING LEAVES YOUR DEVICE.

DATA COLLECTION AND STORAGE

LOCAL-ONLY OPERATION

  • ALL DATA GENERATED BY THIS APP IS STORED EXCLUSIVELY ON YOUR DEVICE
  • NO DATA IS TRANSMITTED TO EXTERNAL SERVERS, CLOUD SERVICES, OR THIRD PARTIES
  • NO ANALYTICS, TRACKING, OR TELEMETRY IS COLLECTED
  • NO INTERNET CONNECTION IS REQUIRED FOR THE APP TO FUNCTION

WHAT WE STORE (ON YOUR DEVICE ONLY)

THE APP MAY STORE THE FOLLOWING INFORMATION LOCALLY ON YOUR DEVICE:

  • APPLICATION SETTINGS AND PREFERENCES
  • USER-CONFIGURED RULES AND FILTERS
  • LOGS AND ACTIVITY RECORDS
  • DATABASE ENTRIES CREATED WITHIN THE APP

ALL OF THIS DATA REMAINS ON YOUR DEVICE AND IS NEVER TRANSMITTED EXTERNALLY.

NETWORK ACTIVITY

NO EXTERNAL COMMUNICATIONS

  • THE APP DOES NOT MAKE ANY NETWORK REQUESTS
  • NO DATA IS SENT TO EXTERNAL APIS OR SERVICES
  • NO BACKGROUND DATA SYNCHRONIZATION OCCURS
  • NO ADVERTISEMENTS ARE SERVED (NO AD SDKS)

PERMISSIONS

THE APP MAY REQUEST CERTAIN DEVICE PERMISSIONS SOLELY FOR LOCAL FUNCTIONALITY:

  • STORAGE PERMISSIONS: TO SAVE DATA LOCALLY ON YOUR DEVICE
  • NETWORK PERMISSIONS: MAY BE REQUESTED FOR PLATFORM REQUIREMENTS, BUT NO ACTUAL NETWORK COMMUNICATION OCCURS
  • OTHER PERMISSIONS: ONLY AS NEEDED FOR CORE APP FUNCTIONALITY, NEVER FOR DATA COLLECTION

DATA CONTROL

YOU HAVE COMPLETE CONTROL

  • ALL DATA REMAINS UNDER YOUR CONTROL AT ALL TIMES
  • YOU CAN DELETE ALL APP DATA THROUGH YOUR DEVICE SETTINGS
  • UNINSTALLING THE APP REMOVES ALL ASSOCIATED DATA
  • NO ACCOUNT CREATION OR PERSONAL INFORMATION IS REQUIRED

NO ACCOUNT REQUIRED

  • THE APP FUNCTIONS WITHOUT REQUIRING ANY ACCOUNT OR REGISTRATION
  • NO PERSONAL INFORMATION IS COLLECTED OR REQUESTED
  • NO EMAIL ADDRESSES, PHONE NUMBERS, OR OTHER IDENTIFIERS ARE GATHERED

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

THIS APP DOES NOT INTEGRATE WITH ANY THIRD-PARTY SERVICES THAT COULD COMPROMISE YOUR PRIVACY:

  • NO SOCIAL MEDIA INTEGRATIONS
  • NO ANALYTICS SERVICES (GOOGLE ANALYTICS, FIREBASE, ETC.)
  • NO CRASH REPORTING SERVICES THAT SEND DATA EXTERNALLY
  • NO ADVERTISING NETWORKS
  • NO EXTERNAL AUTHENTICATION SERVICES

DATA SECURITY

SINCE ALL DATA REMAINS ON YOUR DEVICE:

  • YOUR DATA IS PROTECTED BY YOUR DEVICE'S SECURITY MEASURES
  • NO DATA IS VULNERABLE TO NETWORK INTERCEPTION
  • NO DATA BREACHES CAN OCCUR FROM OUR SIDE (AS WE NEVER RECEIVE YOUR DATA)
  • YOU MAINTAIN FULL OWNERSHIP AND CONTROL OF YOUR INFORMATION

CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

THIS APP DOES NOT KNOWINGLY COLLECT ANY INFORMATION FROM ANYONE, INCLUDING CHILDREN UNDER 13. SINCE NO DATA LEAVES THE DEVICE AND NO PERSONAL INFORMATION IS COLLECTED, THE APP IS SAFE FOR USERS OF ALL AGES.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

ANY CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY WILL BE REFLECTED IN UPDATES TO THIS DOCUMENT. HOWEVER, THE CORE PRINCIPLE REMAINS UNCHANGED: YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE.

CONTACT

IF YOU HAVE QUESTIONS ABOUT THIS PRIVACY POLICY OR THE APP'S PRIVACY PRACTICES, PLEASE REFER TO THE PROJECT REPOSITORY OR DOCUMENTATION.

SUMMARY: THIS APP IS DESIGNED TO WORK ENTIRELY OFFLINE. NOTHING YOU DO IN THIS APP IS SENT ANYWHERE. YOUR DATA IS YOURS ALONE, STORED ONLY ON YOUR DEVICE, AND COMPLETELY UNDER YOUR CONTROL.
